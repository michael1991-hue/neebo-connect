Family-sharing privacy

What the current Nivvi family test sends, stores and lets you control.

Effective 16 September 2026 · Nivvi TestFlight family-test configuration

This notice describes a personal family test using a self-hosted PC. It is not a notice for an approved commercial or medical monitoring service. Public internet access to the server does not mean that Nivvi is ready for public release.

Who to contact

For this family test, contact Nivvi at hello.nivvi@outlook.com about privacy, support or a request concerning your information. A separate legal business identity and service address have not been established for a commercial launch.

Local monitoring

Local monitoring needs no online account. The iPhone stores your profile, chosen avatar, readings, history, settings and notes. The main reading and event history retains today and the previous 29 calendar days. Child photographs are not used in the current build; avatars are used instead.

The app uses Bluetooth and notification permissions. You control these in iPhone Settings. Data kept only on your phone is not sent to the family server unless you turn sharing on.

Same-Wi-Fi share

When both iPhones are on the same home Wi‑Fi, the nursery phone can share the latest heart rate, oxygen where supported, connection status, alert state, charging state and band battery to the downstairs phone using a 4-digit code. That traffic stays on the local network. It does not go through the family server.

Accounts and remote sharing

Each adult uses a separate verified email account. The server stores the email address, a salted password hash, hashed session and verification credentials, family label, membership and hashed invitations. It does not store your plain-text account password.

When you enable sharing on the phone near the wearable, it can send the latest heart rate, oxygen value where supported, measurement timestamp, source description, connection status, alert state and today’s sampled history to the family server at family.nivvi.app. Profile avatars, birth dates and local notes are not included. Use a nickname for the family label.

Who can see the readings

The owner and invited accounts verified at the invitation’s intended email address can view the family’s readings. Invited members have read-only access. This does not allow them to change the host phone’s alarm limits.

The connection uses HTTPS. This is not end-to-end encryption between phones: the server processes readable measurements, and someone with access to the PC or database may access them. A recipient may keep a screenshot or copy.

Services involved

  • Host PC and Docker: the account database and latest snapshot are stored in a Docker volume on the operator’s PC. The PC must stay awake for remote access.
  • Cloudflare Tunnel: provides the public HTTPS route from family.nivvi.app to that PC. Cloudflare processes connection metadata; tunnel traffic is encrypted in transit. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Outlook: support inbox and verification email sending for this test. Anything you email, including attachments, is processed there. See Microsoft’s privacy statement.
  • Apple TestFlight: distributes the iPhone build and processes the Apple ID email used to invite testers. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Vercel: hosts these public information pages. It may process IP addresses and request metadata. These pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, advertising or tracking pixels added by Nivvi. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

Remote Apple push notifications are not configured yet. Local notification permissions on the iPhone are separate.

Retention and deletion

  • The latest uploaded snapshot replaces the previous one and expires after 24 hours while the server is running.
  • Invitations expire after 24 hours, verification/reset codes after 15 minutes, and account sessions after 30 days.
  • Account records remain until the account is deleted.

Stop sharing and remove all access removes the hosted group, its latest snapshot, invitations and member access. Delete online account removes that account and its owned sharing records; it does not delete local iPhone history.

Choices and children’s information

Use this family test only for yourself or someone whose information you have authority to manage. Sharing is optional. Do not invite people without appropriate authority. Do not upload children’s photographs.

To request access, correction or deletion of information handled by the test operator, contact the support address above. You can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Support and changes

Start a support request with a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, API keys, children’s photographs or full health logs unless there is a specific need. Material changes will be explained in the app or on this site.
