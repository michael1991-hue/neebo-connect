Family-sharing privacy

What the current Nivvi family test sends, stores and lets you control.

Effective 14 September 2026 · Nivvi 0.10.1 family-test configuration

This notice describes a personal family test using a self-hosted server. It is not a notice for an approved commercial or medical monitoring service. Public internet access to the server does not mean that Nivvi is ready for public release.

Who to contact

For this family test, contact Nivvi at hello.nivvi@outlook.com about privacy, support or a request concerning your information. A separate legal business identity and service address have not been established for a commercial launch.

Local monitoring

Local monitoring needs no online account. The iPhone stores your profile, optional photo, readings, history, settings and notes. The main reading and event history retains today and the previous 29 calendar days; cleanup happens when the app prepares or writes its stores. Diagnostic captures, exports, older migration copies and phone backups can remain separately.

The app uses the system photo picker and Bluetooth and notification permissions. You control these in iPhone Settings. Data kept only on your phone is not sent to the family server unless it is included in the sharing described below or you choose to export or send it.

Accounts and sharing

Each adult uses a separate verified email account. The server stores the email address, a salted password hash, hashed session and verification credentials, family label, membership and hashed invitations. It does not store your plain-text account password.

When you enable sharing on the phone near the wearable, it sends the latest heart rate, oxygen value where supported, measurement timestamp, source description, connection status and alert state. Uploads are normally limited to about one per ten seconds, with alert-state changes prioritised when the app can run. Sharing must be enabled again after an app relaunch.

Profile photos, birth dates, local notes and the historical readings file are not included in these uploads. Use a nickname for the family label: labels, source descriptions and other text may themselves identify someone.

Who can see the readings

The owner and invited accounts verified at the invitation's intended email address can view the family's readings. Invited members have read-only access. This does not allow them to change the host phone's alarm limits.

The connection uses HTTPS, and the server checks account access. This is not end-to-end encryption between phones: the server processes readable measurements, and someone with authorised administrative access to the PC or database may access them. A recipient may retain a screenshot or copy.

Services involved

  • Host PC and Docker: the account database and latest snapshot are stored in a persistent Docker volume on the operator-controlled server. This setup has not established managed backup retention or a verified disk-encryption arrangement.
  • Tailscale Funnel: provides the public HTTPS route to the PC. Tailscale processes connection and service metadata; its Funnel relay passes encrypted traffic to the PC. See Tailscale's privacy policy.
  • Resend: sends verification and password-reset emails from the verified mail.nivvi.app domain using its Ireland sending region. It processes recipient email addresses, email contents including the temporary code, and delivery records. Health readings are not included in these account emails. A sending region does not establish that all provider processing stays there. See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Outlook: hosts the support inbox. Anything you email, including attachments, is processed there. See Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Vercel: hosts these public information pages and manages the domain. It may process IP addresses and request/security metadata. These pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, advertising or tracking pixels added by Nivvi. See Vercel's privacy policy.

The current AltStore family-test build does not enable remote Apple push notifications. Local notification permissions are separate. A future push-enabled version will need an updated explanation of Apple's role and appropriate provisioning.

Retention and deletion

  • The latest uploaded snapshot replaces the previous one and expires after 24 hours. The server runs cleanup while it is operating; a stopped PC can delay cleanup.
  • Invitations expire after 24 hours, verification/reset codes after 15 minutes, and account sessions after 30 days. Expiry does not imply secure erasure of every physical copy.
  • Account records remain until the account is deleted. Short-lived rate-limit records use hashed network addresses or account identifiers; expired rate-limit records are cleaned up during later rate-limited requests.
  • Provider delivery/security records, support messages, database pages, PC or phone backups and exported copies have separate retention. No complete provider-log or backup-deletion schedule has been verified for this test, so immediate permanent erasure of all copies is not promised.

Stop sharing and remove all access removes the hosted group, its latest snapshot, invitations and member access. Delete online account removes that account and its owned sharing records; it does not delete local iPhone history. Members can leave, and owners can remove members. Access removal is enforced on subsequent requests; values already shown can persist until the next refresh or freshness expiry. It cannot recall copies already made.

You can edit local profile information, remove a profile photo, delete history and manage diagnostic files separately on your iPhone. Phone backups and externally shared exports must be managed separately.

Choices and children's information

Use this family test only for yourself or someone whose information you have authority to manage. Sharing is optional and must be enabled explicitly. You can stop it at any time. Do not include another child's information or invite people without appropriate authority.

The current in-app sharing acknowledgement is not a substitute for completing the operator's assessment of lawful processing, health-data conditions, children's information, provider agreements and international transfers before wider use. These commercial-release decisions remain outstanding.

To request access, correction or deletion of information handled by the test operator, contact the support address above. Identity or authority may need to be checked. Depending on the circumstances, data-protection rights may also include restriction, objection and portability. You can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Support and changes

Start a support request with a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, API keys, children's photographs or full health logs unless there is a specific need and an agreed way to handle them. Any new hosting, analytics, billing, push-notification or sharing arrangement needs this notice reviewed before it is used. Material changes will be explained in the app or release notes.
