Privacy

What Nivvi stores on your iPhone, and what optional family sharing sends to London.

Effective 20 September 2026 · Nivvi 1.0 TestFlight · Michael Waters, trading as Nivvi, United Kingdom.

This is a family test, not a commercial medical service. Contact hello.nivvi@outlook.com.

On your iPhone

Local monitoring needs no account. The app stores the child profile (name, date of birth, optional gender, avatar), heart-rate and oxygen readings where the band supplies them, connection and alert events, notes, alert settings and the chosen Bluetooth identifier.

History keeps today and the previous 29 calendar days. You pick the date on the History tab. Diagnostic capture logs from a manual session are stored separately.

There is no advertising, analytics or AI SDK in this build. iPhone backups may include app data if you allow them.

Same-house Wi‑Fi

A 4-digit PIN on the same Wi‑Fi can send the latest reading to a second iPhone downstairs. That traffic stays on the home network. It does not go through the family server.

Family sharing (optional)

Each adult uses their own email and a password of at least 12 characters. Verification mail is sent with Resend from hello@nivvi.app.

The family lives on a DigitalOcean server at family.nivvi.app (London), reached over HTTPS via Cloudflare. The server stores email, a salted password hash, session tokens, a 6-letter family code, membership and the latest live snapshot (heart rate, oxygen if present, timestamps, connection and alert state). Birth dates, avatars and notes stay on the phone.

Anyone who has the 6-letter code can join that child’s family and see live readings. Treat the code like a house key. Stop sharing with everyone ends the code. Delete account removes the online login, not local history.

The latest snapshot overwrites the previous one and expires after 24 hours. Sessions last 30 days.

Other services

  • DigitalOcean — hosts the family server
  • Cloudflare — DNS and HTTPS for family.nivvi.app and nivvi.app
  • Resend — verification emails
  • Apple TestFlight — app distribution
  • Vercel — these public pages

Your choices

Use family sharing only for a child whose information you have authority to share. You can raise a concern with the UK ICO.
