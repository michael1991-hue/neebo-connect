Using Nivvi

Terms

Nivvi is a TestFlight family test. It is not a medical device.

Effective 20 September 2026. Provided by Michael Waters, trading as Nivvi, United Kingdom. Support: hello.nivvi@outlook.com.

If a child is seriously unwell, seek emergency help. Do not wait for this app.

What it does

Nivvi shows live heart rate from a compatible Bluetooth band on iPhone (iOS 16+). It can keep 30 days of history, play alerts in the app, share downstairs on the same Wi‑Fi, and share with family over the internet using a 6-letter code.

What it does not do

It does not diagnose, treat, or guarantee that an alarm will sound. Bluetooth, Wi‑Fi, 4G, Silent mode, Focus, Low Power Mode and iOS can interrupt readings. Compatibility depends on the band (standard 180D heart-rate service). Apple Watch, Fitbit Air and similar locked wearables usually will not work.

Your responsibility

Only share a child’s readings if you have authority to do so. Keep the family code private. Each adult uses their own login. You must not rely on Nivvi as the only way you look after someone.

Accounts

Optional family accounts use email verification. We may suspend misuse. You can delete the online account in the app; local history on the iPhone is separate.

Law

These terms follow the law of England and Wales. Your mandatory consumer rights are not affected. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded, including death or personal injury caused by negligence.
