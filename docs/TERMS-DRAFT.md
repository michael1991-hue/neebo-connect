## 2. What the app does

Nivvi displays supported Bluetooth heart-rate readings and stores measurement snapshots, event logs and user notes on your iPhone. It is intended for adult users, including parents and authorised caregivers managing a child's profile. Only enter information you have authority to use.

The app supports the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Service. An additional custom decoder is experimental and is clearly identified as such. A Bluetooth connection or a device appearing in the list does not establish measurement compatibility or accuracy. Compatibility may change with device firmware and phone software. Nivvi is an independent app and does not imply endorsement by a hardware manufacturer.

## 3. Readings and test alerts

This prototype has not been clinically validated. It does not diagnose a condition, provide emergency monitoring or replace professional care, direct supervision or a prescribed monitoring plan. Do not change medicines or treatment based on its readings. A reading returning within a configured limit does not establish that someone is well.

High and low test alerts use limits entered by you and a configured duration. Custom-format alerts require separate experimental opt-in. Readings and alerts may be missing, delayed or incorrect because of contact, movement, device format, Bluetooth, battery, phone settings or operating-system restrictions. No sound-through-mute, uninterrupted connection or delivery guarantee is offered. These explanations do not remove any legal duties owed by the operator.

Follow instructions from your care team. Do not delay urgent help while checking the app or contacting support. Built-in supportive text is general guidance, not personalised medical advice.

## 4. Your information and device

Keep access to your phone secure and review information before exporting it. History keeps today and the previous 29 calendar days; snapshots are normally at least 30 seconds apart while valid data arrives. Event records are saved separately. Export important information before it expires or before removing the app. Diagnostic logs and exported files have different retention, explained in the privacy notice.

The app does not currently provide a remote backup or live caregiver sharing service. Phone backups are controlled by your operating-system settings. Changing the app identifier or uninstalling can affect access to local information.
