# Nivvi support video storyboard

Target length: 90 seconds. Use fictional profile data only.

1. **Opening (0-8s)** — Nivvi logo and the line: “Instant alerts for the beats that matter most.” State that Nivvi is a companion record for compatible Bluetooth heart-rate devices, not a medical device.
2. **Profile (8-18s)** — Create a child profile, choose an optional cropped photo, and leave gender optional. Do not show a real child’s name, date of birth or health information.
3. **Connect (18-35s)** — Open Device, tap Scan for devices, favourite the compatible device, and select it. Show the connected state, battery percentage when supplied, and the “waiting for measurements” state when the device is not worn or has no fresh data.
4. **Live reading (35-48s)** — Show heart rate, connection status and the quick action for Live heart rate. Explain that standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Service devices are the primary supported path.
5. **History (48-65s)** — Open History, choose a calendar day, filter Events, drag across the chart to inspect a timestamp and value, then export or delete the record. Show that readings are sampled every 30 seconds for history while every valid sample is used for alarm evaluation.
6. **Alerts (65-78s)** — Configure care-plan low and high limits, test the siren and notification with test data, then show that a fresh in-range reading clears the alarm. Do not create a real child event to demonstrate an alarm.
7. **Safety and sharing (78-90s)** — Explain that readings stay on the iPhone by default, registered-family sharing requires secure sign-in and consent, and urgent symptoms require the family’s clinical plan or emergency services.

End card: “Verify the device, readings, alarms, locked-phone behaviour, reconnection and history before public release.”
